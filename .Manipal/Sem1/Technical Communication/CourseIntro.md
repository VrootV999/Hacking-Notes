# Course Introduction

Technical Communication- DCA1106

## Course Description

Communication is a vital skill that significantly influences personal, academic, and professional success. Technical Communication is designed to strengthen students’ understanding of communication principles while enhancing their proficiency in vocabulary, grammar, and professional correspondence. This course adopts a comprehensive approach to mastering both verbal and written communication. Through structured modules, learners will develop clarity, precision, and confidence in expressing ideas across diverse contexts, preparing them for effective interaction in academic settings and the modern workplace.

## Course Objectives

By the end of the course, students will be able to:
1. Understand the different types, processes, and principles of communication.
2. Apply vocabulary-building strategies for precise and effective language use.
3. Demonstrate grammatical accuracy and avoid common errors in speech and writing.
4. Construct clear, varied, and coherent sentences and techniques of précis writing.
5. Develop professional communication skills required for workplace interactions.
6. Prepare job applications, résumés, and cover letters, and demonstrate readiness for interviews.
7. Enhance public speaking and presentation abilities for effective audience engagement.

## Course Outcomes

After successful completion of the course, students will be able to:
1. Explain key concepts, types, and models of communication and apply them in real-life contexts.
2. Use an expanded vocabulary and appropriate language structures in academic and professional communication.
3. Identify and correct grammatical errors to produce clear and accurate written content.
4. Construct well-organized sentences and write concise, coherent précis of longer texts.
5. Prepare professional documents such as emails, résumés, cover letters, and reports with clarity and correctness.
6. Demonstrate effective interpersonal, public speaking, and presentation skills in academic and workplace situations.
7. Exhibit improved confidence, critical thinking, and communication competence in interviews and group discussions.
