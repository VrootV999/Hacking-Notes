# Course Introduction [Environmental Science (DCA1110)]

## Course Description
The course Environmental Science is designed to cover the fundamentals of Environment Science. 
This COURSE focuses primarily on the study of the environment and how humans interact with it, using a combination of physical, biological, and chemical sciences.
Environmental Science is an interdisciplinary academic field that integrates physics, biology, meteorology, mathematics etc.  
Environmental Science deals with the study of processes in soil, water, air and organisms which lead to pollution or environmental damage and the scientific basis for the establishment of a standard which can be considered acceptably clean, safe and healthy for human beings and natural ecosystem.
The scope of environmental studies is very wide, and it deals with many areas like Conservation of natural resources, ecological aspects, pollution of the surrounding resources and controlling it.
It also deals with various social issues and the impact of human population on the environment.
The environmental studies make us aware about the importance of protection and conservation of our mother Earth and about the destruction due to the release of pollution in the environment.
It has been well recognized that environment issues like ozone depletion and global warming, acid rain, pollution are not merely national issues but are global issues and hence require international efforts and cooperation to solve them.

---
## Course Objectives

- Students will be able to understand the concept of ecosystem.
- Understanding the concept of Biodiversity and its conservation.
- Have a deeper knowledge of different types of pollutions like air, water, soil, and noise pollution.
- Recognize the impacts of environmental damage and pollution.
- Understand the regulatory and legal frameworks that promote sustainability.
- Understand the meaning of population and other terms surrounding this concept.
- Explain resettlement and rehabilitation of populations and issues around them.
- Understand why we need environmental communication and maintain environmental ethics.
- Describe what disasters are and how they can be caused.

---
## Course Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

- Understand the basic concept of environmental science and ecosystem including food chain and food web.
- Analyse the different activities, including pollution which harm the environment and learn the methods to reduce the impact of pollution on environment.
- Understand the different laws issued by the government for protecting the environment.
- Understand the different types of disastrous activities, their cause and harmful effects to both human and environment.
- Describe the different aspects of population growth and understand how the growing population affect the environment.
