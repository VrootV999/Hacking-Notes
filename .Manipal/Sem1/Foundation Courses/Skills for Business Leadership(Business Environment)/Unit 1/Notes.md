#### External Environment
##### Types of External Environment
- Micro Environment
    - Only affect certain aspects of the business but it's still external
    - `Aspects Related to Micro Environment`: Suppliers, Marketing Intermediaries, Customers, Market, Competitors, Financiers
- Macro Environment
    - Affects so many parts of the businesss and it's still external
    - `Aspects Related to Macro Environment`:  
        - `Economic`: price levels, FDI, foreign exchange rate, rules and regulations
        - `Technological environment`: Tech used,decelopments seen in tech 
        - `Political & legal`: Rules and Regulation
        - `Demographic`: final customers, age distribution, populuation, income distribution
        - `Social/Cultural`: relates to the distribution of people in terms of culture and their society
        - `Ecosystem`: reducing pollution, green marketing, taking care of the environment
        - `Global`: Competition from foreign players

# Unit 2: Recognition the Environment
## Importance of Recognizing the Environment
Internal and External Environment affect managerial decision-making.
- `Restrictive business environment`: What are the Restrictions present in the business environment i.e: where they need to stop.
- `Present and Future Viability`: It is for both present and future profits
- `Cost of capital and the cost of borrowing`: The cost of production for the company and hence the profitability of the enterprise.
- `Availability of all key inputs`: For the business to be successful, they should be available. (either import or have those key inputs).
- `Increase in public awareness`: How aware the customers are regarding the company and the products
- `Opportunities for growth and profits`: How well can the company grow and what are the Opportunities available to attain profit.

## SWOT Analysis
- Refers to Strength Weakness Opportunities Threats `(a.k,a The four stratergies for a specific company)`
- Organisational environment has basically two components: Strength and Weakness
- Strength and Weaknesses are part of the internal environment.
- Opportunities and Threats are part of the external environment.
- All of them affect the decision-making of a company.
### Steps to run a SWOT Analysis
1. Asses your market
    Identify the needs of the consumer.
2. Assess your company
    Does the company have the capacity to reduce a certain kind of product that is required by the customers.
3. Assess your Competition
    Is it a monopoly or is there any competitors doing the same.
4. Find the Opportunities available
    can the company do something to make the output better.
5. Identify the Threats
    Can be from any business environment factor or variable.
6. internal Analysis
    Looking at the strength and Weaknesses of the Organisation to see the capabilities of the organisation.
7. External Analysis
    What is the external environment within which the company is operating.
