Cheat Sheet for nvim

B/W tabs
alt + k/j  l/h 

B/W buffers 
TAB or shift+TAB 
create new buffer:    space b 
kill buffer:          space x


QUIT
Save:                   :w
Quit:                   :q 
Not save & Quit:        :q!
Quit all:               :qa!

Terminal
Escape:                 ESC 
Horizontal terminal:    space h 
Vertical terminal:      space v
quit:                   exit

Resize
resize (value):         resize tabs horizontally
vertical resize (value) resize tabs vertically

Mischellanious
Open Cheat Sheet:       space c h
